The "Roles for Foodcoop" module provides a comprehensive role-based
access control system for the Foodcoop platform. It defines multiple
user roles with specific group memberships to ensure proper segregation
of duties and access control.

Pre-defined Roles
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

This module includes 19 distinct roles covering all aspects of Foodcoop
operations:

1.  **Cashier**: Basic POS operations
2.  **Member Manager**: Member lifecycle management
3.  **Accountant**: Financial operations
4.  **Inventory Manager**: Stock control
5.  **Teamleader**: Shift coordination
6.  **Purchaser**: Procurement tasks
7.  **Purchase Manager**: Procurement management
8.  **Foodcoop Admin**: Full system administration
9.  **BDMLecture**: Read-only BDM access
10. **BDMPresence**: Attendance tracking
11. **BDMSaisie**: BDM data entry
12. **POS Manager**: POS management

14. **Communications Officer**: Marketing operations

Usage
=====

Installation
------------

1. Copy this module to your Odoo addons directory.
2. Update the apps list.
3. Install the "Roles for Foodcoop" module.

Configuration
-------------

This module provides pre-defined user roles for the Foodcoop platform:

- **Cashier**: Point of sale operations
- **Member Manager**: Member management and related tasks
- **Accountant**: Accounting and invoicing
- **Inventory Manager**: Stock and inventory management
- **Teamleader**: Shift management coordination
- **Purchaser**: Purchase order management
- **Purchase Manager**: Purchase department management
- **Foodcoop Admin**: Full administrative access
- **BDMLecture**: BDM reading access
- **BDMPresence**: BDM presence tracking
- **BDMSaisie**: BDM data entry
- **POS Manager**: Point of sale management
- **Subscription**: Subscription management
- **Communications Officer**: Email marketing operations
- **Communications Manager**: Email marketing management
- **Welcome Meeting Team**: Event management
- **Member Accountant**: Restricted accounting access
- **BadgeReader**: Badge reading access
- **Staff**: Staff access

Usage
-----

After installation, users are automatically assigned to roles based on
predefined configurations. Each role has specific group memberships that
determine access rights throughout the system.

To assign roles to users:

1. Go to Settings > Users & Companies > Users
2. Open a user form
3. Add role lines in the "Roles" tab
4. Save the user

The system will automatically apply the appropriate groups based on the
assigned roles.
