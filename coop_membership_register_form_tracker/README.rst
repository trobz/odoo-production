Track the UTM source from the membership discovery meeting registration
form.

- Adds UTM source tracking fields to partners (many2many discovery
  meeting sources)
- Extends the discovery meeting registration form to ask how the visitor
  found the cooperative
- Supports grouping sources by category and free-text "other" input
- Adds sequence and category fields to ``utm.source``
