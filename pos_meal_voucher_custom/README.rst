This module customizes the Point of Sale payment screen to automatically
apply a calculated meal voucher amount when the cashier selects a meal
voucher payment method, based on the eligible order amount and the
maximum configured voucher limit.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

To use this module:

1. Configure meal voucher payment methods in the POS settings.
2. Set a maximum meal voucher amount in the POS configuration.
3. In the POS payment screen, click on a meal voucher payment method.
   The voucher amount will be automatically calculated and applied based
   on the eligible order total.
