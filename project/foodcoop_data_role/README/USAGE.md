## Installation

1. Copy this module to your Odoo addons directory.
2. Update the apps list.
3. Install the "Roles for Foodcoop" module.

## Configuration

This module provides pre-defined user roles for the Foodcoop platform:

- **Cashier**: Point of sale operations
- **Member Manager**: Member management and related tasks
- **Accountant**: Accounting and invoicing
- **Inventory Manager**: Stock and inventory management
- **Teamleader**: Shift management coordination
- **Purchaser**: Purchase order management
- **Purchase Manager**: Purchase department management
- **Foodcoop Admin**: Full administrative access
- **BDMLecture**: BDM reading access
- **BDMPresence**: BDM presence tracking
- **BDMSaisie**: BDM data entry
- **POS Manager**: Point of sale management
- **Subscription**: Subscription management
- **Communications Officer**: Email marketing operations
- **Communications Manager**: Email marketing management
- **Welcome Meeting Team**: Event management
- **Member Accountant**: Restricted accounting access
- **BadgeReader**: Badge reading access
- **Staff**: Staff access

## Usage

After installation, users are automatically assigned to roles based on predefined
configurations. Each role has specific group memberships that determine access rights
throughout the system.

To assign roles to users:

1. Go to Settings > Users & Companies > Users
2. Open a user form
3. Add role lines in the "Roles" tab
4. Save the user

The system will automatically apply the appropriate groups based on the assigned roles.
