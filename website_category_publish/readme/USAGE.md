## Publish / Unpublish a category

1. Go to **Website → eCommerce → eCommerce Categories**.
2. Open a category.
3. Click the **globe icon** button at the top of the form to toggle between
   **Published** and **Unpublished**.

When a parent category is toggled, all child categories that have a different
published state are automatically synchronized.

## Effect on the shop

- **Unpublished category**: hidden from the shop sidebar and from the category
  filter.
- **Products**: a product is hidden from the shop if all of its assigned
  categories are unpublished. A product with no category is always visible.
