Allow users to publish/unpublish eCommerce products by their category.

When a category is unpublished:

- All products belonging exclusively to unpublished categories are
  hidden from the shop.
- The category is removed from the shop sidebar.
- All child categories are automatically synchronized to the same
  published state as their parent.

A product is visible on the shop if it has no category assigned, or if
at least one of its categories is published.

Usage
=====

Publish / Unpublish a category
------------------------------

1. Go to **Website → eCommerce → eCommerce Categories**.
2. Open a category.
3. Click the **globe icon** button at the top of the form to toggle
   between **Published** and **Unpublished**.

When a parent category is toggled, all child categories that have a
different published state are automatically synchronized.

Effect on the shop
------------------

- **Unpublished category**: hidden from the shop sidebar and from the
  category filter.
- **Products**: a product is hidden from the shop if all of its assigned
  categories are unpublished. A product with no category is always
  visible.
